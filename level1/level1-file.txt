India is a vibrant, multi-ethnic nation in South Asia, bounded by the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea, and the Bay of Bengal. It stands as the world's most populous country and the seventh-largest by land area. Known for its deep historical roots, India is the birthplace of four major world religions: Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism. This profound spiritual heritage shapes a complex cultural tapestry defined by thousands of distinct languages, colorful festivals, and diverse culinary traditions.
