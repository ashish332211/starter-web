Summer is the hottest and brightest season of the year. It arrives after spring and brings long days and short nights. The sun shines strong in a clear blue sky, filling the world with warmth and vibrant energy.The Beauty of NatureBright Flowers: Gardens burst into full bloom with red, yellow, and pink colors.Sweet Fruits: Juicy mangoes, watermelons, and lychees arrive to delight our taste buds.Lively Outdoors: Birds sing in the trees, and green leaves dance in the warm breeze.
